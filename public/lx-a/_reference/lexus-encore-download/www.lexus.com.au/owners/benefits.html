Lexus DriveCare

Our 24-hour roadside assistance service for Encore Members provides peace of mind, around the clock.

Lexus DriveCare6 services include:

  • Breakdown assistance with fuel, battery or tyre/wheel replacement
  • Basic mechanical assistance
  • Breakdown towing as required to a Lexus Dealer or nominated technical centre
  • Personal Assist service (Terms and Conditions apply)
  • Clothing and personal effects cover up to $250 (including GST)

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become an Encore member?

Enrolment in Encore Owner Benefits is complimentary on delivery of new vehicles and Lexus Certified Pre-Owned vehicles2 purchased on or after 1 January 2020.

Once you have purchased an eligible Lexus vehicle from your preferred Lexus dealership, and shortly after you have taken delivery of your vehicle, your Lexus Dealer will complete your enrolment for Encore Owner Benefits.
How do I become an Encore Elevate member?

Current Lexus owners can apply for Encore Elevate via the Lexus Encore App. Alternatively, you can contact your preferred Lexus dealer to find out more and become a member of Encore Elevate.
How do I become an Encore Platinum member?

Enrolment in Encore Platinum Owner Benefits is complimentary on LC, LS, LX and LM model purchases. 

Once you have purchased an eligible Lexus vehicle from your preferred Lexus dealership, and shortly after you have taken delivery of your vehicle, your Lexus Dealer will complete your enrolment for Encore Platinum Owner Benefits.
Am I eligible for Encore?

You are eligible for Encore if you purchase an eligible Lexus vehicle from your preferred Lexus dealership. Shortly after you have taken delivery of your vehicle, your Lexus Dealer will complete your enrolment for Encore Owner Benefits or Encore Platinum Owner Benefits.

Alternatively, to check if you’re eligible for Encore Owner Benefits or Encore Platinum Owner Benefits, you may contact the Lexus Customer Assistance Centre (Monday to Friday 8:00am to 6:00pm) on 1800 023 009 or complete the Contact Us form.
Am I eligible for Encore Elevate?

Current Lexus Owners who have a Lexus VIN can purchase Encore Elevate. This excludes Encore Platinum Members
How do I access my Encore benefits?

To redeem a Service Loan Car, you must book your standard scheduled logbook servicing with your preferred Lexus Dealer.

To redeem Lexus DriveCare,6 call 1800 253 987.

You can access Exclusive Offers and your Ampol Fuel Offer10 through the Lexus Encore App.

You will receive invitations to Exclusive Events throughout the year if you have provided us with your details and opted in to receiving communication about Encore Owner Benefits. Lexus use your details to notify you of new Exclusive Offers and invite you to Exclusive Events.

To update your contact details, please call our Lexus Customer Assistance Centre (Monday to Friday 8:00am to 6:00pm) on 1800 023 009 or complete the online form.
How do I access my Encore Elevate benefits?

You can access all your Encore Elevate benefits in the Lexus Encore App.
How do I access my Encore Platinum benefits?

You can book a Lexus on Demand through the Lexus Encore App.

You can access Airport Lounge passes through the Lexus Encore App.

You can find more information about Valet Parking and the number of available redemptions in the Lexus Encore App. No pre-booking is necessary.

What will happen with current Encore Platinum Electrified benefits?

Owners of Lexus RZ 450e vehicles produced before November 2023 and Lexus UX 300e vehicles produced before June 2024 will continue to receive their 3 years of Encore Platinum Electrified.5

As at April 2026, Encore Platinum Electrified comprised:
Encore Benefits – Exclusive Offers, Exclusive Events, Service Loan Car and DriveCare6 (5 years for RZ).
Platinum Electrified Benefits – On Demand (Up to 8 days × 4)7, Valet Parking (× 8)11, Airport Lounge (× 4), Charging cables, Home Charger and standard installation8, and complimentary access to Chargefox network.9
How do I access the Lexus Encore App?

Once you have purchased an eligible Lexus vehicle from your preferred Lexus dealership, and shortly after you have taken delivery of your vehicle, your Lexus Dealer will complete your enrolment for Encore Owner Benefits or Encore Platinum Owner Benefits.

You will then receive an email with instructions on to access the Lexus Encore App and complete your registration.

You may access the Lexus Encore App on your mobile device or computer.

Accessing using a mobile device

  1. Download the Lexus Encore App from the Google Play (Android) or App Store (iOS)
  2. Enter your details (First name, Last name, Email, Mobile number) and set a password. Follow the prompts to register
  3. Once your ownership has been verified by Lexus, you will receive a link
  4. Click this link and you will be taken to the login page
  5. Log in using your email and password. You will now be able to access your Encore account

For assistance, please call the Lexus Customer Assistance Centre (Monday to Friday 8:00am to 6:00pm) on 1800 023 009.

Accessing on a computer

  1. To register your account, click here. You can also login on the top right-hand corner of your screen/menu
  2. Enter your details (First name, Last name, Email, Mobile number) and set a password. Follow the prompts to register
  3. Once your ownership has been verified by Lexus, you will receive a link
  4. Click this link and you will be taken to the login page
  5. Log in using your email and password. You will now be able to access to your Encore account

For assistance, please call the Lexus Customer Assistance Centre (Monday to Friday 8:00am to 6:00pm) on 1800 023 009.
How do I update my details?

Lexus use your details to notify you of new Exclusive Offers and invite you to Exclusive Events.

To update your contact details, please call the Lexus Customer Assistance Centre (Monday to Friday 8:00am to 6:00pm) on 1800 023 009 or complete the online form.

03.Elevate Member Benefits can be purchased by all Lexus Vehicle Owners, for a 12-month period from the date Encore is first purchased and activated by the Lexus Vehicle Owner. Inclusions may change. Subject to availability. For full T&C’s and eligible models, see your Lexus Dealer or lexus.com.au/smallprint/encore-terms.

Subject to availability. Current Electrified Platinum Members are not eligible for Encore Elevate. For full T&Cs and eligible models, see your Lexus Dealer or lexus.com.au/smallprint/encore-terms.
06.DriveCare as part of Lexus Encore benefits applies for the term of membership to Lexus Encore, with exceptions applying to certain vehicles, through complimentary DriveCare superseding the term of the Encore Membership. Coverage and service inclusions vary dependent on the type of vehicle and vehicle location. For full T&Cs see your Lexus Dealer or lexus.com.au/smallprint/encore-terms.

Vehicles may vary dependent on location and availability. For full T&C’s, details & eligibility requirements see your Lexus Dealer or lexus.com.au/smallprint/encore-terms.

Vehicle owner must obtain appropriate site permissions and access prior to installation. Electricity consumption charges are owner's responsibility. Refer to operating instructions for usage guidance. Upgrades and equipment add-ons at additional cost. Rainchecks not available. Cannot be redeemed for monetary value. See jetcharge.com.au for T&Cs and lexus.com.au/smallprint/encore-terms for details.

Usage at vehicle owner’s risk. See chargefox.com for T&Cs. Frequent use of DC Fast Charging can impact battery performance & durability. Lexus recommends minimising DC Fast Charging usage.
